Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Solana Snake ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Solana Snake mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Solana Wallet Address: If you choose to connect a Solana wallet using Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA), we receive your wallet's public key (public address). This is a public blockchain identifier — it is not a private key and does not grant us any access to your funds.

Connecting a wallet is entirely optional. The game is fully playable without connecting a wallet.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Local Game Data: Your high score is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data through third-party analytics SDKs

2. How We Use Information

The wallet public address, if provided, is used solely to display a shortened version in the app's score bar (e.g., "Ab12…Xy34") as a visual indicator that your wallet is connected. It is not stored on any server, shared with third parties, or used for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage and Security

All game data (high score) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store personal data. We do not transmit any user data over the internet, except for the standard network requests required by the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter protocol to communicate with your locally installed wallet app.

4. Blockchain Interactions

When you connect a wallet, the Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) protocol facilitates communication between the App and your wallet app installed on the same device. Any transactions or message signing requests will always require your explicit approval within your wallet app. We never initiate transactions without your consent.

Please note that blockchain transactions are public and permanent by nature. Any on-chain activity associated with your wallet address is visible on the Solana blockchain.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party libraries and services:

  • Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter — for wallet connectivity (open source, no data collection)
  • @solana/web3.js — for Solana blockchain interaction (open source)
  • Expo / React Native — application framework (open source)

We do not integrate any advertising networks, analytics platforms, or data brokers.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
